    Notes and assumptions

    For a closer look. The cards above are the summary.

    What the numbers include

    • Income is an estimated long-term rent from the Oct 28, 2026 HiCentral Matrix 96814 1-bed lease pull, using a building or size band stated on each card and in Full numbers.
    • Costs taken out: vacancy 5% (assumption); HOA; property tax as shown on the listing source; and HO-6 insurance of $45 per month (assumption, from a published Hawaii condo average of $540 per year as of Sep 30, 2026 on insurancequotes.com).
    • Closing costs, reserves, special assessments, utilities not included in HOA, and furniture replacement are not in the cash-in or expense figures.
    • Cash Return (Cap Rate) = yearly net income ÷ price. With all cash there is no loan, so cash-on-cash and cap rate are one number. Closing costs are not in it. For the down-payment view, monthly cash flow is after principal and interest at the rate in Filters, and cash-on-cash is annual cash flow ÷ the down payment. The default rate is the Freddie Mac 30-year fixed average of 7.11% as of Nov 12, 2026 (PMMS).
    • 10-year appreciation uses sold data where it could be sourced, and is otherwise labeled as an assumption. Ala Moana Hotel 1-beds: about 2.8% per year from a thin same-plan sample (#2226 sold $310,000 on Jun 30, 2016; 2026 Matrix solds #907 at $375,000 and #1207 at $445,000). Kapiolani Manor 1-beds: about −1.9% per year from a thin public sample (#909 sold $320,000 on Mar 11, 2016; 2026 public 1-bed solds around $240,000–$270,000). Summer Palace and Atkinson Plaza: about 2.5% per year from the Honolulu County condo median of $390,000 in 2016 (Hawaii DBEDT Data Book) versus the Oahu condo median of $499,000 in October 2026 (Honolulu Board of REALTORS Monthly Statistical Report). A building median for 2016 versus 2026 was not in the HiCentral pull, which only went back to October 2024 for hotel solds. Past appreciation does not predict future prices.
    • *Est. total return = Cash Return + 10-year yearly appreciation. It is an estimate for comparing properties, not a forecast or a promise.
    • Investor Score: each metric is ranked against the other properties on this page (0 = weakest, 100 = strongest) and weighted: Cash Return 35, est. total return 20, monthly net cash flow 10 (using the down payment and rate in Filters), gross rent multiplier 10 (lower better), expense ratio 10 (lower better; yearly operating expenses ÷ yearly gross rent; vacancy is applied separately in net income), 10-year appreciation 10, price per unit 5 (lower better; each listing is one home).
    • Not included: furnishing or renovation, special assessments, depreciation or tax benefits. This is not tax, legal, or lender advice, and it is not a guarantee of income, expenses, or future value.

    Before an offer

    • Confirm current status, price, HOA, and tax on the official listing, and ask for building documents and a rent history.
    • Hotel-program income at Ala Moana Hotel can differ from the long-term rent band, and long-term leasing of a hotel-program unit may be limited.
    • Cards use market-rent midpoints from the lease comps above unless a card’s Full numbers say otherwise. Atkinson Plaza #1006 is larger than the 550–587 sq ft lease comps in that building, so the $2,400–$2,600 band may be conservative. Summer Palace #2006 has a tenant through the end of April 2027; the current lease amount is not in the source data, so market rent is used.
